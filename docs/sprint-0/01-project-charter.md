# Peer Circles — Project Charter

## 1. Project Information

**Project Name:** Peer Circles

**Project Type:** Mobile Peer-Support Platform

**Module:** SE3080 – Agile Software Development / Scrum Project

**Team Size:** 4 members

**Development Methodology:** Scrum

**Project Duration:** Sprint 0 + Sprint 1–4

---

## 2. Project Background

Peer Circles is a mobile peer-support platform designed to provide users with a safe and supportive environment where they can discover peer-support communities, participate in group discussions, share experiences and communicate with other members.

The project is based on the user research and UI/UX work completed for the related UEE project. The research identified the importance of community interaction, support groups, privacy and accessible communication.

---

## 3. Problem Statement

People who experience personal, emotional or social difficulties may benefit from connecting with others who have similar experiences. However, finding suitable peer-support communities and participating in them through a structured digital platform can be difficult.

Peer Circles aims to provide a centralized mobile platform where users can discover relevant peer-support groups, join communities, participate in discussions and communicate with other members while maintaining appropriate privacy considerations.

---

## 4. Proposed Solution

Peer Circles will provide a mobile application that allows users to:

- Register and log into an account.
- Manage their profile.
- Discover available peer-support groups.
- Search for relevant support groups.
- View group information.
- Join support groups.
- Participate in group discussions.
- Create discussion posts.
- Comment on posts.
- Use privacy-conscious participation features.
- Communicate with other members through planned communication features.

---

## 5. Project Goal

The goal of the project is to develop a functional mobile peer-support platform using Scrum and demonstrate the complete Agile development process through iterative development, testing, stakeholder feedback and continuous improvement.

---

## 6. Project Approach

The team will use Scrum with Sprint 0 followed by development sprints.

The team will maintain a Product Backlog, Sprint Backlogs and a Definition of Done. Development work will be tracked through GitHub.

The team will conduct Scrum events including:

- Sprint Planning
- Daily Scrum
- Sprint Review
- Sprint Retrospective

Scrum responsibilities will rotate between team members across the development sprints.

---

## 7. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is a functional mobile application demonstrating the core Peer Circles user journey, supported by:

- Working application features
- Backend services
- Database integration
- Functional testing
- Scrum documentation
- GitHub project history
- Sprint evidence
- Final project documentation

---

## 8. Major Constraints

The project has the following constraints:

- Limited development time.
- Four-person student development team.
- Limited time available for implementation and testing.
- Features must be prioritized according to their value and feasibility.
- The application is a peer-support platform and is not intended to provide professional medical diagnosis or emergency intervention.

---

## 9. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if:

1. The core application can be executed successfully.
2. Users can register and authenticate.
3. Users can manage their basic profile.
4. Users can discover and interact with peer-support groups.
5. Users can participate in discussions.
6. Core functionality is tested.
7. Major defects are resolved before final submission.
8. Scrum activities and team collaboration are documented.
9. Each team member has identifiable contributions.
10. The final product and documentation are completed within the assignment deadline.
