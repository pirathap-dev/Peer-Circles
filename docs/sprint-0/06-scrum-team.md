## 3. Product Owner Responsibilities

The Product Owner is responsible for:

- Maintaining the Product Backlog.
- Prioritizing requirements.
- Clarifying user stories.
- Defining or approving acceptance criteria.
- Representing product value.
- Reviewing completed increments.
- Managing stakeholder feedback.

---

## 4. Scrum Master Responsibilities

The Scrum Master is responsible for:

- Facilitating Scrum events.
- Supporting the Scrum process.
- Identifying and helping remove blockers.
- Supporting effective team collaboration.
- Monitoring Scrum practices.
- Helping maintain transparency of sprint progress.
