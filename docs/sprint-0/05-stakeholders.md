# Peer Circles — Stakeholders

## 1. Stakeholder Identification

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest | Influence | Main Concern |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Peer-support users | Primary users | High | High | Usability, privacy and useful communities |
| Project team | Developers | High | High | Successful implementation and quality |
| Product Owner | Product representative | High | High | Product value and requirements |
| Scrum Master | Process facilitator | High | Medium | Effective Scrum process |
| Module lecturer/evaluator | Academic stakeholder | High | High | Scrum practices, technical quality and evidence |
| Project supervisor | Academic/project stakeholder | High | High | Project progress and quality |
| Community/support stakeholders | Potential domain stakeholders | Medium | Medium | Appropriate peer-support functionality |

---

## 2. Primary Stakeholder

The primary stakeholder group is the intended Peer Circles users.

Their needs influence the Product Backlog and feature priorities.

---

## 3. Academic Stakeholders

The module lecturer/evaluator and project supervisor are responsible for evaluating the project according to the assignment requirements and expected Agile/Scrum practices.

---

## 4. Stakeholder Feedback

Stakeholder feedback will be considered during Sprint Reviews.

Relevant feedback may result in:

- New backlog items.
- Changes to existing user stories.
- Changes in priority.
- UI/UX improvements.
- Changes to acceptance criteria.

The Product Owner is responsible for maintaining product priorities based on stakeholder needs and available project capacity.
