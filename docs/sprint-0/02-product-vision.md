# Peer Circles — Product Vision

## 1. Product Vision

To provide a privacy-conscious mobile peer-support platform that enables people to discover relevant support communities, connect with peers, share experiences and participate in supportive discussions in a structured digital environment.

---

## 2. Target Users

The primary users of Peer Circles are individuals who are interested in participating in peer-support communities and communicating with people who have similar experiences or interests.

---

## 3. User Needs

Based on the existing user research, users need:

- Easy registration and login.
- A simple way to discover relevant support groups.
- The ability to search for communities.
- Information about groups before joining.
- The ability to participate in group discussions.
- The ability to create posts and comments.
- Privacy-conscious participation.
- A manageable user profile.
- Accessible communication with other members.

---

## 4. Product Value

Peer Circles aims to provide value by bringing peer-support communities and communication features together in one mobile platform.

The platform focuses on accessibility, community participation, privacy and ease of use.

---

## 5. Product Vision Statement

For individuals seeking peer support and community interaction,

Peer Circles is a mobile peer-support platform

that enables users to discover communities, join support groups and participate in meaningful discussions,

unlike disconnected communication channels,

because Peer Circles provides a structured environment specifically designed around peer-support community participation.
