# Peer Circles — Product Backlog

## 1. Backlog Prioritization

The Product Backlog is prioritized based on user value, project requirements, complexity and available development capacity.

Priority levels:

- Critical — essential for the core product.
- High — important functionality.
- Medium — valuable but can be implemented after core functionality.

## 3. Sprint 1 Backlog

Sprint 1 will focus on the foundation of the application.

### Sprint Goal

Build the authentication and basic profile functionality required for users to access Peer Circles.

### Selected Stories

- US-01 — Register
- US-02 — Login
- US-03 — Logout
- US-04 — Profile

### Total Estimated Story Points

11 story points.

The team will review the Sprint Backlog during Sprint Planning and may adjust the selection based on capacity and technical dependencies.

## 5. Estimation Method

The team will use story points to estimate relative effort and complexity.

A Fibonacci-style scale will be used:

1, 2, 3, 5, 8

Story points consider:

- Complexity.
- Development effort.
- Technical uncertainty.
- Dependencies.
- Testing effort.

The estimates will be discussed and agreed upon by the team rather than assigned by an individual member.
