## Overall Project Retrospective (Sprints 1–4)

As the final sprint of the project, the team conducted a retrospective on the entire Scrum and Agile development journey:

### 1. Scrum Role Rotation
- Rotating Product Owner and Scrum Master roles across all 4 team members gave every individual hands-on experience in backlog management, requirement negotiation, event facilitation, and obstacle removal.
- Each member developed a stronger appreciation for both engineering execution and Agile process management.

### 2. Velocity and Estimation Evolution
- **Sprint 1 (11 points):** Focused on foundational authentication; learning curve regarding Git and coordination.
- **Sprint 2 (23 points):** High-velocity surge implementing core community features; tested team limits on workload and query complexity.
- **Sprint 3 (15 points):** Balanced scope allowed deep focus on complex privacy and safety logic.
- **Sprint 4 (13 points):** Polished extensions, notifications, and full system regression testing.
- The team became markedly more accurate in estimating story points, identifying technical dependencies early, and avoiding scope creep.

### 3. Engineering & Collaboration Practices
- Defining API request/response contracts and data schemas upfront eliminated backend-frontend friction.
- Integrating QA testing early in every sprint cycle rather than waiting for development completion significantly boosted code quality.
- Disciplined Git branching strategy with feature branches, code reviews, and pull requests maintained repository stability.

## Retrospective Conclusion
Sprint 4 concludes the development phase of Peer Circles on a high note. The team completed all planned user stories, satisfied the Definition of Done across every increment, and delivered a secure, responsive, and functional mobile peer-support platform.

The experience gained in applying Scrum principles, managing technical debt, communicating transparently, and continuously improving sprint-over-sprint provides an invaluable foundation for future collaborative engineering endeavors.
